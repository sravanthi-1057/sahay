About SAHAY

Turning confusion into clear action. SAHAY is a problem-first civic guidance platform built to help people find support without needing to know service titles.

OUR OBSERVATION

The Problem We Observed

“There are many services and support systems available, but finding the right one can be difficult.”

We saw an opportunity to make that journey simpler and clearer.

Citizen Challenges

  • Which service is relevant to their situation
  • Whether they are eligible
  • What documents they need
  • Where they need to apply
  • What steps they need to follow
The Defining Question

“What if people could just explain their problem, instead of figuring out what service they need first?”

THE SPARK

How the Idea Came About

SAHAY started with a simple question.

That idea became the foundation of SAHAY.

Instead of making people search through complicated categories and unfamiliar service names, SAHAY aims to understand what they need and guide them toward relevant support.

BRAND STORY

Why the Name SAHAY?

The name SAHAY represents the idea behind the platform — help, support, and guidance when you need it.

We wanted a name that reflected what we hope SAHAY becomes: a starting point that helps people move from a problem toward the right direction.

OUR MISSION

“Our mission is to make finding the right service simple, accessible, and understandable for everyone.”

We believe no citizen should miss out on education grants, healthcare coverage, or civic resolution simply because they didn't know the exact government title.
