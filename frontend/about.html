About SAHAY

SAHAY helps people discover the right services based on their problems rather than requiring users to know the exact service name.

OUR MISSION

“Our mission is to make finding the right service simple, accessible, and understandable for everyone.”

We believe no citizen should miss out on education grants, healthcare coverage, or civic resolution simply because they didn't know the exact government title.
