PROCESS EXPLAINED

How SAHAY Works

SAHAY simplifies the process of finding and accessing the right service by starting with your situation, not government jargon.

Try SAHAY Now
Five Sequential Steps

The Complete SAHAY Journey

How we take you from confusion to an actionable official resolution.

01 — Tell Us

01 — Tell Us

Describe your problem naturally in your own words. You don't need to know the official name of the service, scheme, or department.

Example: “I can't afford my college fees for this semester.”
02 — Understand

02 — Understand

SAHAY asks a few relevant clarification questions to understand your academic level, income band, or location.

Example: “Are you pursuing undergraduate studies?”
03 — Match

03 — Match

SAHAY identifies schemes, grants, certificates, or municipal dispatch services that may be relevant to your problem.

Example: Matched with Post-Matric Scholarship & Fee Waivers.
04 — Guide

04 — Guide

Understand exact eligibility criteria, prepare required documents in advance, and review numbered application steps.

Example: Aadhaar, Income Proof, College ID, Fee Receipt.
05 — Act

05 — Act

Go to the verified official government portal and complete your submission with confidence.

Example: Visit scholarships.gov.in portal.
