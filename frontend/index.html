Core Philosophy

What is SAHAY?

Start with the problem, not the service name.

SAHAY is an intuitive problem-to-service discovery platform. Whether a student is struggling with college fees, a resident faces garbage accumulation, or a farmer seeks crop support, SAHAY translates everyday human situations into clear, actionable official directions.

The Problem: Citizens know their struggle, but don't know the official scheme name or bureau department.
The SAHAY Solution: Describe your situation naturally → Get matched schemes, eligibility, required documents, and verified application links.
