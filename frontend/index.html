Core Philosophy

What is SAHAY?

SAHAY is a platform that helps you find the right service or support for your needs. Simply tell us what you’re facing in your own words — you don’t need to know the name of the service.

SAHAY helps you discover relevant services, check your eligibility, understand the documents you need, and know the next steps to take.

From education and healthcare to government services, employment, housing, and civic needs, SAHAY helps turn confusion into clear action.

Start with your problem. Find your way forward.
